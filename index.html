To Nora

Eighteen looks so good on you.

I wanted you to have something today that's just yours to open — no cake required, though I hope there's plenty of that too. Eighteen is a big one. It's the birthday where the door actually opens: new keys, new choices, a whole stretch of road that's officially yours to walk.

Whatever this year brings, I hope it hands you exactly the kind of joy you deserve — the loud kind, the quiet kind, and everything in between. I'm just glad I get a front-row seat to it.

Here's to the girl turning eighteen today, and to every version of you still to come.

Happy birthday, Nora. I love you.
